Data Deletion

How to delete your data from MyFitnessTracker. You are always in complete control.

Last updated: May 2025
🛡
KEY FACT

MyFitnessTracker stores all data locally on your device or in your personal Firebase database. There are no user accounts, no cloud databases that we control, and no servers that hold your information. This means you can delete all your data instantly, at any time, directly from within the app or by uninstalling it.

01

What Data MyFitnessTracker Stores

On your device (local only):

Food Logs — Meals you've logged are stored in a local SQLite database on your device.

Workout Sessions — Exercise history, sets, reps, and weights are stored locally.

Weight Entries — Your weight tracking history is stored locally.

Habit Data — Wellness habits and streaks are stored locally.

App Preferences — Settings, theme preferences, and goals are stored locally.


In Firebase (optional cloud backup):

If you choose to use cloud sync, your data is stored in your own personal Firebase Firestore instance. This data is only accessible to you and is not visible to us.

02

Deleting Data From Inside the App

TO DELETE INDIVIDUAL FOOD ITEMS:

  1. Open the app and go to the Meal Diary screen.
  2. Find the food item you want to delete.
  3. Tap the delete icon or swipe left on the item to remove it.

TO DELETE INDIVIDUAL WORKOUT SESSIONS:

  1. Open the app and go to the Workout Diary screen.
  2. Tap View History on any exercise.
  3. Find the session you want to delete and use the delete option.

TO DELETE WEIGHT ENTRIES:

  1. Open the app and go to the Progress screen.
  2. Your weight history is shown in the chart. Individual entries can be deleted from the weight log.

TO CLEAR ALL SCAN HISTORY:

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Clear History or Clear All Data.
  3. Confirm the deletion. This removes all local data permanently.
03

Deleting All Data by Uninstalling

Uninstalling MyFitnessTracker from your device will permanently delete all locally stored data, including all food logs, workout history, weight entries, habits, and app settings.

If you have cloud sync enabled, your data will remain in your personal Firebase instance. You can delete it from there, or it will be automatically removed after 30 days of inactivity.

  1. Long-press the MyFitnessTracker icon on your home screen or app drawer.
  2. Tap Uninstall and confirm.
  3. All local data is permanently erased.
04

Clearing App Data via Android Settings

You can also clear all stored data without uninstalling the app through Android's built-in storage settings:

  1. Go to your device's Settings → Apps.
  2. Find and tap MyFitnessTracker.
  3. Tap Storage.
  4. Tap Clear Data. This permanently deletes all local app data.
05

Deleting Firebase Cloud Data

If you have enabled cloud sync, your data is stored in your own personal Firebase instance. To delete this data:

  1. Log in to the Firebase Console.
  2. Navigate to your project's Firestore Database.
  3. Delete the collections associated with your user ID.
  4. Alternatively, uninstalling the app and not using it for 30 days will auto-clean your data.

If you need assistance, contact us and we can help guide you through the process.

06

No Account or Personal Data Stored by Us

MyFitnessTracker does not require an account and does not collect personal information. There is no user profile, no cloud-synced data we control, and no database entry associated with you personally on our servers.

The steps above cover all data that MyFitnessTracker stores. You are always in complete control of your information.
